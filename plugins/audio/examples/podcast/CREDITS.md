## About this example

Original script (agentatelier). A two-host cold open, then the theme comes in under the show intro and goes out after it. NOVA's laugh is the voice's own (three joined native laugh tags); the line took two retakes because the laugh swallowed the word after it.

- Rendered by `produce.mjs` on 2026-10-11 with Supertonic 3 (supertonic 1.3.1); QA clean (8 segments).
- `mix.mp3`: LAME VBR quality 2 from mix.wav, as produce.mjs writes it (decoded true peak -1.21 dBTP, sample peak -1.22 dBFS).
- Sounds were fetched with their license sidecars, then trimmed, faded or looped as noted above; they are not committed (only the script, mix, captions, timeline, credits and preview are). The script's `./sounds/` paths point to those local files.
