[scene: The old oak tree, bedtime]  [ambience: ./sounds/crickets.mp3 | low]
[music: start]
NARRATOR (tender): High in the old oak tree, a little owl called Pip could not sleep.
PIP (nervous): Mama says owls love the dark. But I don't. Not even a little bit.
NARRATOR: Then, just past the end of her beak, something tiny began to glow.
[sfx: ./sounds/twinkle.mp3 | low]
GLOW (happy): <laugh> Hello! I'm Glow. I light up when I'm happy. Watch!
PIP (surprised): You're a star? A tiny, flying star?
GLOW (excited): Even better. I'm a firefly! And the dark is my favourite place to shine.
PIP (scared): But what if something's out there?
GLOW (tender): Then we'll look at it together. Look up.
NARRATOR (calm): Pip looked up. And there, above the branches, a thousand little lights were blinking hello.
PIP (happy, slow): Oh. The dark is full of friends.
[sfx: ./sounds/owl.mp3 | low]
PIP (tired): <yawn> Goodnight, Glow.
NARRATOR (tender, slow): And with Glow beside her, Pip closed her eyes. Goodnight, little owl.
[music: out]
