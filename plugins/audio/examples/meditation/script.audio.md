---
title: Two-minute breathing break
lang: en
preset: meditation
cast:
  GUIDE: { voice: F3 }
music: { path: ./sounds/pad.mp3, level: -14dB }
---
[scene: Arrive]  [ambience: ./sounds/stream.mp3 | low]
[music: start]
[sfx: ./sounds/bowl.mp3 | -14dB]
[pause 2]
GUIDE (tender): Welcome. For the next two minutes, there is nothing you need to do.
GUIDE (tender): Let your shoulders soften. Let your hands rest, wherever they are.
[pause 2]
[scene: Breathe]
GUIDE: We'll breathe together. In through the nose, for four. Then out, slowly, for six.
[pause 1.5]
GUIDE (calm): Breathe in.
[pause 4]
GUIDE (calm): And let it go. Slowly.
[pause 5]
GUIDE (calm): Again. Breathe in.
[pause 4]
GUIDE (calm): And out.
[pause 5]
GUIDE (tender): If a thought arrives, that's fine. Notice it, and let it float past, like a leaf on the water.
[pause 2]
GUIDE (calm): Breathe in.
[pause 4]
GUIDE (calm): And out.
[pause 5]
GUIDE (calm): One more. In.
[pause 4]
GUIDE (calm): And all the way out.
[pause 5]
[scene: Return]
GUIDE (tender): Feel the weight of your body, and the ground holding you up.
[pause 3]
GUIDE (tender): When you're ready, take one easy breath, and open your eyes.
[pause 1]
[sfx: ./sounds/bowl.mp3 | -14dB]
[music: out]
