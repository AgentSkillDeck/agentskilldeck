## About this example

Text: Arthur Conan Doyle, A Study in Scarlet (1887), part 1, chapter 1, public domain in the US and wherever the term is life plus 70 years or less; from Project Gutenberg eBook #244. Abridged to one scene; abbreviations are spelled out for the voice (Dr. as Doctor, Mr. as Mister), "reagent" is written re-agent so it is not read as one syllable, and the British haemoglobin is kept. Mastered to ACX: RMS -20.1 dB, peak -3.5 dBFS, noise floor -71.9 dB, with room tone at head and tail so the noise-floor gate has real silence to measure.

- Rendered by `produce.mjs` on 2026-10-11 with Supertonic 3 (supertonic 1.3.1); QA clean (15 segments).
- `mix.mp3`: re-encoded from mix.wav at 80 kbps CBR to stay under 1 MB (produce.mjs writes 192 kbps CBR for ACX) (decoded true peak -3.51 dBTP, sample peak -3.51 dBFS).
- Sounds were fetched with their license sidecars, then trimmed, faded or looped as noted above; they are not committed (only the script, mix, captions, timeline, credits and preview are). The script's `./sounds/` paths point to those local files.
