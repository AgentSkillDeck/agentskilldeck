[scene: Chapter One, Mr. Sherlock Holmes]
NARRATOR: This was a lofty chamber, lined and littered with countless bottles. Broad, low tables were scattered about, which bristled with retorts, test-tubes, and little Bunsen lamps, with their blue flickering flames.
NARRATOR: There was only one student in the room, who was bending over a distant table absorbed in his work. At the sound of our steps he glanced round and sprang to his feet with a cry of pleasure.
HOLMES (excited): I've found it! I've found it!
NARRATOR: he shouted to my companion, running towards us with a test-tube in his hand.
HOLMES (excited): I have found a re-agent which is precipitated by haemoglobin, and by nothing else.
NARRATOR: Had he discovered a gold mine, greater delight could not have shone upon his features.
STAMFORD: Doctor Watson, Mister Sherlock Holmes,
NARRATOR: said Stamford, introducing us.
HOLMES (happy): How are you?
NARRATOR: he said cordially, gripping my hand with a strength for which I should hardly have given him credit.
HOLMES (calm): You have been in Afghanistan, I perceive.
NARRATOR (surprised): How on earth did you know that?
NARRATOR: I asked in astonishment.
HOLMES (happy): <laugh:1> Never mind,
NARRATOR: said he, chuckling to himself.
