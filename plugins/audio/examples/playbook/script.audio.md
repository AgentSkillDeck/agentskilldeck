[scene: Before you start]  [ambience: ./sounds/factory.mp3 | -30dB]
TRAINER (authoritative): Lock out, and tag out. Six steps, every time, before you service a machine.
[scene: Step one, prepare]
TRAINER: Step one. Prepare. Know every energy source on this machine: electrical, hydraulic, air, and anything stored in springs or raised parts. Tell everyone nearby that it is going down.
[scene: Step two, shut down]
TRAINER: Step two. Shut it down with the normal stop control.
[scene: Step three, isolate]
TRAINER: Step three. Isolate it. Open the main disconnect, and close the supply valves.
[scene: Step four, lock and tag]
TRAINER: Step four. Lock and tag every isolation point. Your lock, your tag, your key. Nobody else's.
[sfx: ./sounds/padlock.mp3 | wait]
[scene: Step five, release stored energy]
TRAINER: Step five. Release stored energy. Bleed off pressure in the lines, block or lower raised parts, and let capacitors discharge.
[scene: Step six, verify]
TRAINER: Step six. Verify. Press start. Nothing should move. Then return the control to off.
TRAINER (authoritative, slow): If anything moves, stop. Step away, and report it.
